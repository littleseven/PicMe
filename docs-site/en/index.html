FAQ

Are my photos uploaded to the cloud?

No. All AI and face processing runs on your device. Your photos never leave your phone.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases — every feature is free.

Does it work offline?

The on-device AI (Qwen) works offline. The remote AI (DeepSeek) is optional and requires a network.

Which devices are supported?

Android 7.0 (API 24) and above. Beauty and on-device inference run best on arm64 devices.

Is any data collected?

No. We collect no user data. See the Privacy Policy.
