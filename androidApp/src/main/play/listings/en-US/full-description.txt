PoLang is a private AI photo gallery that finds, organizes, and edits the photos and videos on your phone. Browse like a regular gallery — then go further: semantic photo search, on-device AI tagging, people grouping, a chat assistant that manages your gallery in plain language, and a full photo editor. Free, no ads, no subscriptions.

SEARCH PHOTOS IN PLAIN LANGUAGE
Type or say what you remember — "sunset at the beach", "dog playing in the snow", "photos of my daughter last summer". PoLang combines on-device semantic search (CLIP-class image understanding) with auto tags, face grouping, and time & place filters, so vague descriptions and precise queries both work. You can also search by image: send a reference photo to find visually similar shots.

ORGANIZE AUTOMATICALLY, ON YOUR DEVICE
Photos are tagged on-device by scene, people, objects, and activities. Browse this photo gallery by tag or by person — name a face once, then just ask for "mom's photos" or "pictures of my kids". Duplicate and similar photos are found for you, ready to clean up.

CHAT WITH YOUR GALLERY
The built-in AI assistant answers questions about your collection and acts on it: find photos, summarize your gallery, organize media — always with your confirmation before anything is changed or deleted.

EDIT LIKE A PRO
A full photo editor with beauty retouch (skin smoothing, face shaping), filters, one-tap cutout & background eraser, and standard ID photos with a clean background — or simply tell the assistant "make it warmer" and watch the edit happen.

PRIVACY FIRST, OPEN SOURCE
All photo tagging, face detection, and semantic search run 100% on your device. Your photos are never uploaded. The full source code is open on GitHub — verifiable, not just promised.

GOOD TO KNOW
• First run: download the on-device AI models (~1.5 GB, Wi-Fi recommended), then run one gallery scan (~1.5 h per 10,000 photos; an optional deeper content pass can run overnight while charging). Already-scanned photos are skipped, so later scans are fast.
• AI chat uses a remote large language model and needs internet. Only text is sent — never your photos. Free quota included; register with email for a higher quota.
• Android 7.0+; arm64 devices recommended for on-device AI.

PoLang is free, with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases — a private, AI-powered photo gallery for searching, organizing, and editing your photos.
