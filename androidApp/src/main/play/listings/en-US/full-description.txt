PoLang is a photo gallery app that helps you organize, find, and edit the photos and videos on your phone. It works like a regular gallery — browse, view, and manage your media — and adds an on-device AI assistant that tags your photos automatically, so you can search and manage them in plain language.

What you can do with PoLang:

• Browse your gallery
View all photos and videos in a fast, clean gallery. Photos are automatically tagged on your device by scene, people, objects, and activities, and you can browse them by tag or by person.

• Search photos in plain language
Instead of scrolling, type or say what you are looking for — "sunset photos", "screenshots", "pictures of my dog". PoLang matches your words against the on-device tags and shows the right photos instantly.

• Chat with the AI assistant
A built-in chat assistant answers questions about your gallery and helps you act on it: find photos, summarize your collection, or organize media — with your confirmation before anything is changed or deleted.

• Take photos with the beauty camera
A built-in camera with real-time beauty preview (skin smoothing, face shaping) and live filters, powered by on-device face tracking.

• Edit photos
Adjust and enhance photos with the built-in editor: beauty retouch, filters, and one-tap cutout that can also create standard ID photos with a clean background.

• Keep your photos private
All photo tagging, face detection, and search run entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to a server for processing.

PoLang is free, with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases.

In short: PoLang is a private, AI-assisted photo gallery for finding, organizing, and editing your photos on your phone.
