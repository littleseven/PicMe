🖼️ Smart AI Photo Gallery
AI auto-tags each photo by scene, people, objects, and activity. Browse your album by People, Places, or Things, run natural-language photo search, and let AI build smart albums. Finding any photo takes a single sentence.

🔍 Natural-Language Photo Search
Stop scrolling. Just ask: "photos from last summer", "sunsets", "screenshots". On-device AI understands your intent and instantly surfaces matching photos — no internet required, and your images never leave your phone.

GPU-accelerated photo editing: skin smoothing, whitening, face slimming, eye enlarging, lip color, blush, with precise left/right control. One-tap AI enhancement analyzes each photo and recommends the best settings — or just describe the look you want.

Real-time beauty preview with a near-zero-lag shutter (<50 ms). Frame-sync face tracking keeps makeup locked in, even during fast movement.
